Privacy Policy — The 8th Seal

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Studio: Forgotten Pixels Studio ("we", "us"), United States

Contact: Forgottenpixelsthe8thseal@gmail.com

1. The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your saved games stay on your device.
  • We only receive information from you if you choose to press SEND on a bug report, or if you play online co-op (explained below).

2. What stays on your device

Saved games, settings (volume, full screen, filters) and, if you write one, unsent bug reports are stored in the game's data folder on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or deleting that folder removes them.

3. Bug reports (only if you press SEND)

When you use REPORT A BUG and press SEND, the game sends us:

  • what you typed, the kind of problem you picked, and your email address only if you typed one;
  • the game version and when you sent it;
  • device facts that help us reproduce problems: operating system and version, device model, processor, graphics card, memory, screen size, language, whether a touch screen or controller is connected;
  • where you are in the game (land, position, health, quest progress, bosses defeated), and a short list of the last things that happened;
  • the end of the game's technical log file (it contains no personal information we know of);
  • a small screenshot of the game — you can switch this off on the report screen.

We use this only to find and fix problems and to reply to you if you left an email address. Reports are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive (Google LLC) and are visible only to the people who work on the game. We keep reports for up to 24 months, then delete them. Do not put passwords or other secrets in a report.

4. Online co-op (only if you host or join a party)

Online co-op connects the players directly to each other (a peer-to-peer connection made with the Godot engine's built-in ENet networking). There is no server of ours or of anyone else in the middle. If you host or join a co-op game, the other players' devices receive the hero name you choose, your position and actions, and game events, so everyone sees the same world, and they can see your IP address (the host's address is what an invite code contains). To let friends connect, the game asks your router to open a network port (UPnP). While you host, your device also announces the game (your hero name, version and number of players) to your local network once a second so friends on the same network can find it. The connection is not encrypted. Do not use your real name as a hero name, and only play with people you know. We do not receive or store co-op traffic. If you prefer, do not use online co-op.

5. Platforms

If you get the game through Steam or Google Play, those stores collect their own data about your purchase and play under their own policies (Valve: store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement; Google: policies.google.com/privacy). We receive only what they choose to share with us, such as sales and crash statistics.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to Forgottenpixelsthe8thseal@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA) you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Write to Forgottenpixelsthe8thseal@gmail.com with your report ID (shown on screen after you send a report) and we will answer within 30 days. Our legal basis for handling bug reports is your consent (you pressed SEND) and our interest in fixing the game.

8. Security

Bug reports travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our collector. No system is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, say so in the game's update notes.
